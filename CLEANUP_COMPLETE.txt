                      COMMON PROBLEMS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ Turns too early?
   → Increase INTERSECTION_MIN_WIDTH to 150-160

❌ Misses intersections?
   → Decrease INTERSECTION_MIN_WIDTH to 120-130

❌ Turns too sharp?
   → Reduce turn angles by 3-5 degrees

❌ Turns too gentle?
   → Increase turn angles by 3-5 degrees

❌ Wrong signboard detected?
   → Increase SAMPLES_REQUIRED to 35-40
